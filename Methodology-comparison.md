it's advantage for RP-Cloud labs is to Clear phases make the project easy to plan and manage and it's disadvantage is to Changes to requirements can be difficult and costly once development has started.

this one also for advantage it's An early model can help students and lecturers explain what they need before the full system is developed. where for disadvantage Repeated changes to the prototype may take extra time and money.

## 4.Spiral:

this methodology it's advantage for RP-Cloud Labs is that The team can identify and manage risks during each development cycle. while disavantage is that Risk analysis and repeated cycles may require more time, expertise, and budget.

The team can use Agile if members have the necessary technical skills and communicate regularly,seeking support where their expertise is limited.
