# Stakeholder Groups

**Contributor:** Mukwiye Boris

## Introduction

Stakeholder groups are the people, organizations, or groups who are affected by the software system, use it, provide information for it, or have an interest in its success.

## Main Stakeholder Groups

### 1. Students / Users
Students are the main users of the system. They interact with the system to access its features and complete their required activities.

### 2. Lecturers / Teachers
Lecturers provide academic requirements, guidance, feedback, and may use the system to support teaching, learning, or assessment.

### 3. Project Team / Developers
The project team is responsible for designing, developing, testing, documenting, and maintaining the system.

### 4. School / Institution Management
Institutional management is interested in whether the system meets institutional requirements and improves existing processes.

### 5. System Administrators
System administrators manage user accounts, system settings, data, security, and technical maintenance.

### 6. IT Support Team
The IT support team provides technical assistance and helps solve infrastructure and system-related problems.

### 7. Future / External Users
Depending on the project, other users or organizations may benefit from or interact with the system in the future.

## Importance of Identifying Stakeholders

Identifying stakeholder groups helps the development team understand different users' needs and expectations. It ensures that requirements are collected from the appropriate people and helps the team develop a system that addresses the actual problems it is intended to solve.
